# HR Security Policy (HRS)

## 1. Purpose
This policy details the security requirements associated with the human resources lifecycle, ensuring that employees and contractors understand and adhere to Acme Cloud Solutions' security standards.

## 2. Onboarding and Background Checks
- **Background Checks**: Comprehensive background checks are mandatory for all employees and contractors prior to their start date, in accordance with local laws.
- **NDAs**: All personnel must sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and Acceptable Use Policies during the onboarding process.

## 3. Training and Awareness
- **General Training**: All employees must complete security awareness training upon hire and quarterly thereafter. This covers topics such as phishing, password hygiene, and data handling.
- **Specialized Training**: Software engineers and product developers receive specialized secure coding training annually, focusing heavily on mitigating the OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities.

## 4. Operational Security
- **Clean Desk Policy**: Employees are required to ensure that all sensitive information, whether in physical or digital form, is secure when away from their workstations.

## 5. Termination and Exit Procedures
Upon the termination of employment or contract, whether voluntary or involuntary:
- All system access, physical badges, and VPN accounts must be revoked within 2 hours of the termination event.
- All company-owned equipment must be returned immediately.
