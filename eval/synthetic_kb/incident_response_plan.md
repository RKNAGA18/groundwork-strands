# Incident Response Plan (SEF)

## 1. Overview
The Acme Cloud Solutions Incident Response (IR) Plan establishes a structured approach for handling security incidents to minimize impact, restore operations rapidly, and prevent recurrence.

## 2. Incident Response Phases
Our IR process follows a standard 4-phase methodology:
- **Preparation**: Establishing policies, tools, and training.
- **Detection & Analysis**: Continuous monitoring and event correlation to identify potential incidents.
- **Containment, Eradication & Recovery**: Isolating affected systems, removing threats, and restoring normal operations.
- **Post-Incident Activity (Lessons Learned)**: Analyzing the incident to improve future response efforts.

## 3. Monitoring and SLAs
- Our Security Operations Center (SOC) provides 24/7 continuous monitoring of all critical infrastructure and applications.
- **Severity Classification SLA**: All security alerts are triaged and classified for severity within 1 hour of detection.
- **Customer Notification**: For Priority 1 (P1) critical incidents impacting customer data or service availability, customers will be notified within 24 hours of confirmation.

## 4. IR Team Roles
The core Incident Response Team consists of:
- **IR Commander**: Leads the overall response, coordinates activities, and makes critical decisions.
- **Technical Lead**: Directs the forensic investigation, containment, and recovery technical efforts.
- **Communications Lead**: Manages internal and external communications, including legal and PR liaisons.

## 5. Testing
The Incident Response Plan is tested and updated through annual tabletop exercises involving key stakeholders from IT, Security, Legal, and Executive Management.
